# Code of conduct

ActionPlan Kit welcomes contributors of every background, identity and experience level.
Participation in issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces requires respect.

- Discuss code and ideas constructively. Criticize the work, never the person.
- Respect differing experiences and viewpoints. Accept feedback and correct mistakes.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, make unwanted sexual advances, or publish private information.
- Do not spam or repeatedly disrupt a discussion after being asked to stop.

Akshay Nikhare (@akshaynikhare) maintains these standards. Depending on severity and repetition,
responses may include a request to correct behavior, removal of content, a warning, or a temporary
or permanent restriction from project spaces. Decisions should explain the violated rule and use
proportionate consequences.

For public incidents, flag the relevant content to the maintainer without repeating abusive or
private details. For confidential reports or abuse involving the maintainer, use
[GitHub's reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
GitHub handles those reports. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.

These standards are informed by the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
