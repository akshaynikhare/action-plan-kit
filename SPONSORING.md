# Sponsor ActionPlan Kit

Support Akshay Nikhare's open-source work through
[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/akshaynikhare).

GitHub shows the available one-time and recurring options on the sponsorship page. Contributions
help support ongoing maintenance, documentation, testing and improvements to ActionPlan Kit.

You can also help by reporting reproducible bugs, improving guides, testing an upgrade, submitting
a focused pull request, or sharing the project with someone who would find it useful.
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for ways to contribute and [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) for help.

The kit is available under the [MIT license](LICENSE). Its license terms do not depend on sponsorship.
